The first build of the tuner meant for other people to try.

## Install

Download `tuner-1.0.0-beta.apk` below on an Android phone (Android 8.0 or newer), open it and
allow the installation. If an earlier test build of the tuner is on the phone, uninstall that
first: this build is signed with a different key and Android will not install one over the
other.

## What it does

- Tunes by microphone. Pluck a string; the note, how far off it is in cents, and the frequency.
- **Automatic** recognises the instrument from the strings you play (guitar, bass, ukulele,
  violin, viola, cello out of the box) and then shows its strings; tap one to stay on it.
  Chromatic is always available.
- **Smart filter**: once a string is recognised the tuner listens only for that string's
  overtones, which keeps noise and the other strings out of the reading.
- **Waterfall** under the needle: the last eight seconds of the string's spectrum on the same
  scale, so you see how far a turn of the peg moved it.
- The needle shows **how far the reading can be trusted**: sharp for one clear note, a soft
  glow when the signal is weak, noisy, or not alone.
- Settings: waterfall as spectrogram or plain line, needle response (fast, normal, steady),
  and the list of tunings, which you can switch off or copy and edit into your own.

## Known limits

- Checked against recordings of one acoustic guitar through a laptop microphone, and on one
  phone by hand. Other instruments and phones are what this beta is for.
- Fretted notes are shown as the nearest open string in instrument mode; use Chromatic for them.
- Late in a very low note the octave can flip in Chromatic mode.
- A note that wavers by five cents or more loses most of its line in the waterfall.
- In a noisy room stray readings still flick the needle and the note name, though no longer
  the scale or the waterfall's line.

Reports are welcome as issues, ideally with a short recording of what the tuner heard.
